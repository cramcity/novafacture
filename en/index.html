Frequently asked questions

Is it really free?

Yes. The generator is free, with no sign-up and no limits. It is funded by sales of the optional Kit Freelance Pro.

Where is my data stored?

Only in your browser (local storage). Nothing is sent to a server. If you clear your browser data, it is erased.

What must a Canadian invoice show?

Among other things: your business name, the date, the total, your GST/HST registration number if registered, the tax charged, a description of the goods or services, and the buyer's name for sales of $500 or more. Check with the Canada Revenue Agency for your situation.

Do I have to charge GST/HST?

In Canada, registration is generally required only once your taxable sales exceed $30,000 over four consecutive calendar quarters (small supplier). Choose "small supplier (no tax)" if you are not registered. When in doubt, ask an accountant.

Can I make a quote?

Yes: choose "Quote / estimate". The due date becomes the date the offer is valid until.
