How to write an invoice, step by step

A clear, complete invoice gets paid faster and keeps your records clean. Here is the method in 6 steps; exact requirements vary by country and tax status.

Create my invoice for free

  1. Identify yourself — Your name or business name, address, contact details and any tax registration number (e.g. GST/HST in Canada, VAT number in the UK or EU, ABN in Australia).
  2. Identify the client — Their name and address. In Canada the buyer's name is required for sales of $500 or more.
  3. Number and date it — A unique, sequential invoice number (e.g. 2026-001, 2026-002) and the issue date. Add the service date if different.
  4. Describe the work — One line per service or product: description, quantity, unit price and amount. Precise descriptions avoid questions and delays.
  5. Add tax and total — Subtotal, then each tax with its rate (e.g. GST 5%, UK VAT 20%, Australian GST 10%), then the total. If you're not registered for tax, don't charge it.
  6. Set payment terms — The due date, accepted payment methods and, if it's your policy, late fees (only if agreed in advance or allowed where you work). A clear due date, such as 15 or 30 days, avoids any ambiguity.

Frequently asked questions

Can I write an invoice without a registered business?

Yes. A sole proprietor or freelancer can invoice under their own name. You only add a tax number and charge sales tax once you are registered, for example after passing $30,000 of taxable sales over four consecutive calendar quarters in Canada, or £90,000 of VAT-taxable turnover in the UK.

Do I need invoicing software?

No. A free generator, a Word or Excel template works, as long as every invoice has a unique number, the required details and you keep a copy.

When should I send an invoice?

On delivery of the work, or at the milestones set in your contract. For long projects, ask for a deposit and send progress invoices rather than one large bill at the end.

How long should I keep my invoices?

Usually several years: 6 years in Canada, generally 5 to 6 years in the UK, and at least 3 years in the US (longer in some cases). Keep a PDF copy of every invoice you send.

Tip

Send the invoice on delivery day as a PDF, and follow up politely the day after it's due.
