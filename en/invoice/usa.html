What your invoice must include

  • your name or business name, address and email
  • the client's name and address
  • a unique invoice number, the invoice date and the due date
  • a clear description of each service, quantity and rate
  • the total due and accepted payment methods
  • state or local sales tax only if your state taxes your service

Sales tax on services varies by state: check your state's department of revenue. Keep copies of invoices for your tax return.
