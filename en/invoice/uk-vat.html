If you are VAT-registered in the UK (mandatory above £90,000 of taxable turnover over 12 months), your invoices must be valid VAT invoices. Here is an example and the checklist.

What your invoice must include

  • a unique, sequential invoice number and the invoice date
  • your name, address and VAT registration number
  • the customer's name and address
  • the time of supply (tax point) if different from the invoice date
  • a description of the services, the net amount, the VAT rate and the VAT amount
  • the total amount including VAT

Not VAT-registered? Choose "No tax" and do not show VAT. For late business payments you can claim statutory interest (8% above the Bank of England base rate) plus fixed compensation.
