What your invoice must include

  • your business name, the date and a unique invoice number
  • a description of the services
  • GST and QST amounts, with your GST and QST registration numbers
  • the client's name for sales of $500 or more
  • the total and the payment terms

Not registered (small supplier, under $30,000 over four calendar quarters)? Choose "small supplier (no tax)" in the generator.
