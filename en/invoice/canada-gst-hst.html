What your invoice must include

  • Under $100: your business name, the invoice date, the total amount paid, and the GST/HST charged (or a statement that it is included)
  • $100 to $499.99: also your GST/HST registration number and the tax amount or rate
  • $500 or more: also the buyer's name, the terms of payment, and a description of the goods or services

Example uses Ontario HST (13%). Choose your province in the generator: GST 5%, HST 13–15%, or Quebec GST + QST.
