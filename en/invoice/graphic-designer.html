What your invoice must include

  • your business name and contact details
  • the client's name and project name
  • each deliverable as a separate line
  • included and extra revisions
  • when copyright transfers (usually on full payment)
  • the due date and payment methods

Tip: ask for a 30–50% deposit before starting brand identity work, and state the number of included revisions in your agreement.
