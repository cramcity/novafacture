What your invoice must include

  • the words "Tax invoice" and the date of issue
  • your identity and your ABN
  • a description of what you sold, the quantity and the price
  • the GST amount (or a statement that the total includes GST)
  • for sales of A$1,000 or more: the buyer's identity or ABN

Not registered for GST? Do not charge GST and do not call the document a tax invoice. Choose "No tax" in the generator.
