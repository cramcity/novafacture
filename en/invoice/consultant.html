What your invoice must include

  • your business name and contact details
  • the client's name and the purchase order or contract number
  • hours worked (attach a timesheet for hourly billing)
  • fixed-fee items as separate lines
  • the due date (15 or 30 days) and late fee policy
  • accepted payment methods

Tip: referencing the client's purchase order number is often the difference between being paid in 15 days and in 60.
