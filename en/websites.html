How it works

  1. Order: secure payment by PayPal or card.
  2. Questionnaire: you instantly receive a short questionnaire (business, services, photos, contact details). Send it back by replying to the confirmation email.
  3. Draft: first version online within 5 business days of receiving the completed questionnaire.
  4. Revisions: you request changes, we apply them.
  5. Launch: on your domain name, or on a free address if you don't have one yet.

Full refund if work has not started. The site and its copy are yours once paid.

Questions

Do I need to pay for hosting?

Not necessarily: a business website can be hosted for free. Only the domain name (e.g. mybusiness.com) costs money, bought directly from a registrar in your name.

Can you write the copy?

Yes: from your questionnaire we write clear copy in English or French. You approve it before launch.
