# NovaFacture — free invoice & quote generator (FR / EN)

**Générateur gratuit de factures, devis et reçus en PDF — sans inscription, vos données restent dans votre navigateur.**
**Free invoice, quote and receipt generator — no sign-up, your data stays in your browser.**

👉 **Use it / Utiliser :** https://cramcity.github.io/novafacture/ · English: https://cramcity.github.io/novafacture/en/

## Features / Fonctions
- Invoices, quotes and receipts as PDF — factures, devis et reçus en PDF
- 20+ tax presets: Canada (GST/HST, Quebec QST), UK VAT, Australia GST, USA, France (VAT 20 %, micro-entrepreneur art. 293 B), Belgium, Switzerland, Morocco, Côte d'Ivoire, Senegal, Cameroon, DR Congo, custom rate
- Required legal mentions pre-filled per country — mentions obligatoires selon le pays
- "My documents": history, paid / overdue status, payment reminders by email, receipts, client address book, duplicate for next month, CSV export, JSON backup
- Payment link + QR code on the invoice, deposit received / balance due
- 100 % client-side (HTML + vanilla JS, localStorage). No server, no tracking, no account.
